How it works

Your daily service flow in four simple steps

This is the same path your team runs every shift — from the floor plan you saw above to the printed receipt at the counter. Setup once, then repeat every service.

  1. Step 1

    Map your floor & dining areas

    Create table layouts by zone — hosts know exactly where to seat each party.

  2. Step 2

    Open the table & take the order

    Add guest count, pick dishes from the menu, and send to kitchen from the floor view.

  3. Step 3

    Serve & update table status

    Mark tables occupied or billing so hosts and cashiers always know what is next.

  4. Step 4

    Settle the bill & free the table

    Print the VAT receipt, close the check, and turn the table green for the next guests.
